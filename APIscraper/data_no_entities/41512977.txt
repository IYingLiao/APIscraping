41512977|t|Why Magnetic Resonance Imaging Is Invaluable for the Evaluation of Thymic Masses.
41512977|a|The widespread and ever-expanding use of computed tomography for disease, trauma, cardiac imaging, and lung cancer screening has led to increased incidental detection of indeterminate thymic nodules and masses on CT. Chest MRI provides increased diagnostic specificity and delineation of disease extent and should therefore both reduce unnecessary thymectomy and improve clinical management of thymic masses.
